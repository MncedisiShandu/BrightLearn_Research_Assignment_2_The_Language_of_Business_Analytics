# BrightLearn_Research_Assignment_2_The_Language_of_Business_Analytics
The Language of Business Analytics

This repository contains Research Assignment 2 for the BrightLearn Business Analytics course.   The assignment serves as a foundational introduction to the language of business analytics, requiring students to research and define core industry terminology. It covers essential domains including foundational analytics concepts, data measurement and reporting, customer and sales KPIs, and marketing campaign analytics.
